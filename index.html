About

दृष्टि-IO began with a simple question: what if a girl walking home never had to feel alone? Most safety tools need signal, accounts and time she doesn't have in that moment.

So we built a friend that runs on what is already in her hand. It sees who is close, notices when something is wrong, and calls on the people nearest to her.
